\# Business Requirements



\## Business Problem



The e-commerce company needs an interactive Power BI dashboard

to monitor sales, profitability, customer behavior, product

performance, regional performance and returns.



\## Objectives



\- Monitor overall sales and profit performance

\- Analyze customer purchasing behavior

\- Identify top and underperforming products

\- Compare regional and state-level performance

\- Analyze product returns

\- Track monthly and yearly sales trends

\- Support data-driven business decisions



\## Key Business Questions



1\. What are the total sales and profit?

2\. Which categories generate the highest sales?

3\. Which products generate the highest profit?

4\. Which customers contribute the most revenue?

5\. Which regions perform best?

6\. What is the return rate?

7\. Which categories have high return rates?

8\. How is sales performance changing year over year?
